# Investment solution image sources

The three active solution-card images are stored locally in `public/images` and are not hot-linked.

- `solution-fixed-note.png` — user-supplied Nigerian one-thousand-naira banknote image, added locally for the Starfetch Fixed Note card.
- `solution-discretionary-portfolios.png` — user-supplied business-handshake and financial-growth image, added locally for the Starfetch Discretionary Portfolios card.
- `solution-execution-mandate.webp` — adapted from the Pexels photo **“A person holding a black pen”**: https://www.pexels.com/photo/a-person-holding-a-black-pen-7731402/

The remaining stock-library asset was selected from its free-use library and optimised locally as a WebP asset for this frontend.
